# Farm Biosecurity Protocols & Sanitation Checklists

Comprehensive biosecurity protocol manual designed to prevent disease entry and transmission across farm sheds.

---

## 1. Perimeter Zones & Controlled Access
1. **Outer Zone (Farm Gate)**:
   - Mandatory tire wash bath with broad-spectrum disinfectant.
   - Visitor logbook recording prior poultry contact within last 72 hours.
2. **Intermediate Zone (Feed Bins & Service Lanes)**:
   - Dedicated service footwear and disposable overshoes.
   - Prohibition of wild bird and rodent harborage.
3. **Inner Clean Zone (Poultry Sheds)**:
   - Deep footbaths refreshed daily with approved quaternary ammonium or glutaraldehyde solution.
   - Shed-specific boots and coveralls.
