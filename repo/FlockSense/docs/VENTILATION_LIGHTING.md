# Poultry House Ventilation Engineering & Lighting Schedules

Mathematical models and guidelines governing environmental control systems in commercial broiler housing.

---

## 1. Minimum Ventilation Equations
Minimum ventilation removes moisture, carbon dioxide, and ammonia while bringing in fresh oxygen without chilling birds:

$$\text{CFM}_{\text{min}} = \text{Bird Count} \times \text{Weight (kg)}^{0.66} \times \text{Ventilation Constant}$$

---

## 2. Tunnel Cooling & Evaporative Pads
- **Air Speed**: 2.5 to 3.0 m/s throughout the bird level to provide optimal wind-chill effect during warm weather.
- **Static Pressure**: Maintain between 0.10 to 0.15 inches of water column (25 to 37 Pa) depending on house width.
