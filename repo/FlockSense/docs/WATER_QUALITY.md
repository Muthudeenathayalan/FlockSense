# Water Quality Standards & Sanitation Guidelines

Drinking water management protocols to maintain flock gut health and maximize medication delivery efficacy.

---

## 1. Chemical & Microbial Thresholds

| Parameter | Target Range | Corrective Action |
|:----------|:------------:|:------------------|
| pH        | 6.2 - 6.8    | Acidify with citric or organic acid if > 7.5 |
| Free Chlorine | 3 - 5 PPM | Adjust chemical metering pump |
| ORP (mV)  | > 650 mV     | Indicates sterile water line conditions |
| Total Bacteria | < 1,000 CFU/mL | High-pressure hydrogen peroxide line flush |
| Coliforms | 0 CFU/100 mL | Immediate shock chlorination required |
