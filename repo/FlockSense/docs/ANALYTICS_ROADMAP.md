# Poultry Analytics & AI Intelligence Roadmap

Strategic roadmap for predictive analytics, computer vision, and machine learning features in FlockSense.

---

## Phase 1: Statistical Benchmarking (Current v1.2)
- Real-time EPEF calculation against Ross 308 & Cobb 500 standards.
- Water-to-feed consumption anomaly alerting.
- Mortality velocity tracking and epidemic surge prediction.

---

## Phase 2: Predictive FCR & Harvest Weight Modeling (Q1 2027)
- Gradient-boosted decision trees predicting harvest weight at Day 35 based on Day 7 and Day 14 weights.
- Early warning system for sub-clinical coccidiosis based on subtle changes in water consumption curves.
