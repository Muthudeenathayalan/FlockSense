# Poultry Farming Terminology & Abbreviations Glossary

Reference guide for agronomic, physiological, and technical terms used throughout FlockSense.

---

## Glossary Entries

- **ADG (Average Daily Gain)**: Average daily weight increase per bird, measured in grams/day:
  $$\text{ADG} = \frac{\text{Current Weight} - \text{Initial Weight}}{\text{Age in Days}}$$
- **Brooding**: The critical first 7-10 days of a chick's life requiring supplemental heating, high relative humidity, and 23-hour lighting.
- **CV% (Coefficient of Variation)**: Measure of flock weight uniformity. A CV < 8% represents high uniformity.
- **EPEF / PEF (European Production Efficiency Factor)**: Universal industry benchmark combining liveability, growth rate, and FCR into a single index.
- **FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio)**: Total kg of feed consumed divided by total kg of live biomass produced. Lower is superior.
- **ORP (Oxidation-Reduction Potential)**: Measure of water sanitation oxidizing capacity in millivolts (mV).
